Source conditions

Reference friction rate: 0.08 IWC per 100 ft

Use the selected velocity in flex duct for the junction-box table.

Source conditions require sidewall connections, straight approaches and departures, and exit spacing of at least two duct diameters from the entrance.

A nearby turn or inadequate spacing can make the listed losses too small. Adding bend EL does not correct an incompatible box layout.

The optional supplied bend is 90° and uses its own velocity and R/D selections.
